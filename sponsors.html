Sponsorship Opportunity

Celebrate Tradition. Build Visibility.

Your brand can become a meaningful part of one of Murshidabad's cherished community celebrations.

Protappur Baroari Durga Puja Committee has been carrying forward a tradition of devotion, culture and community participation since 1948.

Our annual Durga Puja celebration brings together devotees, families, residents and visitors in a shared celebration of Maa Durga, cultural heritage and community spirit.

We invite businesses, organisations, institutions and individuals to partner with us for the upcoming celebration.

Sponsorship support helps us organise the festival, preserve our traditions, improve arrangements and continue community-oriented activities.
