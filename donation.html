Every Contribution Counts

Protappur Baroari Durga Puja Committee has been serving the community since 1948.

The continued support of devotees, villagers, well-wishers and visitors helps us preserve our religious, cultural and social traditions.

Your contribution can help support Durga Puja celebrations, temple maintenance, religious activities, cultural programmes and community initiatives.
