Frequently Asked Questions

Need More Information?

Here are some commonly asked questions by devotees and visitors.

🛕 Is the temple open throughout the year?

Yes. Shri Shri Durga Temple remains open throughout the year. Special timings may apply during festivals.

📷 Is photography allowed?

Photography is generally allowed outside the temple premises. During rituals, please follow the committee's instructions.

🙏 Can I donate to the temple?

Yes. Donations are welcome for temple maintenance, festivals and social activities. Please visit our Donation page for more information.

🌸 Which festivals are celebrated?

Sharadiya Durga Puja, Kojagari Lakshmi Puja, and Sri Rama Navami are celebrated every year.
