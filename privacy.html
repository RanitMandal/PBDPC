Privacy Policy

How PBDPC collects, uses and protects your information.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Protappur Baroari Durga Puja Committee ("PBDPC", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard information when you visit our website, contact us, or make a donation.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact form, donation form, or by emailing/calling us — such as your name, phone number, email address, and any message or donation details you share.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form.
  • To process and acknowledge donations or sponsorships.
  • To share updates about events, festivals and committee activities, where you have consented to receive them.
  • To improve our website and services.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with trusted committee members solely for the purpose of coordinating puja activities, donations, or sponsorships.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site, so we can improve content and navigation. These do not collect sensitive personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request that we correct or delete personal information you have shared with us.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at info@pbdpc.org or through our Contact page.
