About PBDPC

A Heritage of Faith, Unity & Service

Protappur Baroari Durga Puja Committee (PBDPC), established in 1948, is one of the oldest community Durga Puja committees in Murshidabad.

Over the decades, the committee has preserved religious traditions while promoting social harmony, cultural values and community welfare.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Objectives

For more than seven decades, PBDPC has remained committed to preserving our religious heritage while promoting unity, culture and social responsibility.

Our Mission

To preserve the rich traditions of Durga Puja, promote spiritual values, encourage community participation, and serve society through religious, cultural and humanitarian activities.

Our Vision

To become a model heritage temple committee that inspires future generations by preserving our history while embracing modern technology for community engagement.

Our Objectives

  • Preserve religious traditions.
  • Organize annual festivals.
  • Promote social harmony.
  • Encourage youth participation.
  • Preserve historical records.
  • Serve society with devotion.

Our Journey

Heritage Timeline

A glimpse into the remarkable journey of PBDPC from its humble beginning to the present day.

1948

Foundation

Protappur Baroari Durga Puja Committee was established by local devotees with a vision of celebrating Durga Puja collectively.

1950–2000

Growth of Tradition

Annual Durga Puja became a significant cultural and religious gathering, strengthening community unity.

2021

New Temple Inauguration

The newly constructed temple was inaugurated by Padma Shri Swami Pradiptananda Ji Maharaj (Kartik Maharaj), marking a historic milestone.

Present

Continuing the Legacy

PBDPC continues to preserve religious traditions while embracing digital transformation and community development.
